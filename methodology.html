Methodology

How did we choose the brands?

We selected 64 widely recognized consumer brands across eight industries — restaurants, retail, tech, fashion, shipping, food & CPG, and auto — each with a well-documented visual history stretching from 1970 through today. For every brand, we gathered each publicly available version of its logo.

How did we determine complexity scores?

Each logo was scored on two dimensions: structural detail (how much line work, edges, and shapes it has) and color complexity (how many colors it uses and how saturated they are). These combine into a single complexity score comparing a brand's earliest logo to its most recent one — negative means the logo simplified over time, positive means it grew more elaborate.

How did we sort by color?

For the Color Space chart, each logo version needed one representative color. We identified the most visually dominant, distinctive color in that logo's palette, then used it to place the logo by hue (left to right) and lightness (top to bottom).
